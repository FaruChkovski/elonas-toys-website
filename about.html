About Us

Elona's Toys is your one-stop kids store in Khaldeh, Lebanon — right beside Rammal Supermarket. We believe every child deserves toys that spark joy, imagination, and learning.

From cuddly teddy bears to educational STEM kits, we handpick quality toys for ages 0–12 Months through 9+ Years. We deliver across Lebanon and accept Whish Money or Pay Upon Delivery.
